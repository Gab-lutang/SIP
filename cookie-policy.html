Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how ANZENAI Security Suite uses cookies and similar technologies to provide, customize, evaluate, improve, and secure our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our site.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

  • Enable basic website functionality
  • Allow secure navigation
  • Remember your session state

Analytics Cookies

  • Track website usage patterns
  • Improve site performance
  • Enhance user experience

Preference Cookies

  • Remember your settings
  • Store display preferences
  • Personalize your experience

Cookie Management

You can control and manage cookies in various ways:

  • Modify your browser settings to accept or reject cookies
  • Adjust cookie preferences through our website settings
  • Delete cookies manually through your browser settings

Third-Party Services

Our website uses limited third-party services that may set cookies:

  • GitHub (for source code repository)
  • Font Awesome (for icons)
