About ANZENAI

Pioneering the Future of Digital Security

ANZENAI is a groundbreaking security initiative developed by Grade 12 STEM students from St. Dominic Academy of Pulilan Inc. Combining innovation with academic excellence, our mission is to revolutionize digital security through artificial intelligence and cutting-edge technology.

The name "ANZEN" (安全), meaning "safety" or "security" in Japanese, combined with "AI" represents our commitment to creating a safer digital world through artificial intelligence.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI enhance security?

Our AI system uses advanced machine learning algorithms to continuously monitor and analyze system behavior, detecting and preventing security threats in real-time. It learns from new threats and adapts its protection strategies accordingly.

What systems are supported?

Anzenai Security Suite is compatible with Windows systems and requires Python 3.8 or higher. We're actively working on expanding support to other operating systems.

How is my data protected?

We use AES-256 encryption for all sensitive data, including quarantined files. Our secure key management system ensures that your data remains protected at all times.

Does it slow down my system?

No, ANZENAI is designed to be lightweight and efficient. Using only 6.8MB of storage and less than 1% CPU usage, it runs seamlessly in the background without impacting your system's performance.

How often are updates released?

We release regular updates to enhance security features and address new threats. The AI model is continuously learning and adapting, while software updates are released monthly with critical security patches provided as needed.
